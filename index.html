Compare roofing options

The right material depends on your property, budget and priorities. An inspection is the best way to find the fit.

Asphalt shingles

A widely used, familiar choice for homes.

  • Good fit for many budgets and home styles
  • Lifespan depends on climate, ventilation and installation
  • Routine checks after storms keep small issues small

Metal roofing

A longer-lasting option for owners planning to stay.

  • Higher upfront cost, often fewer replacements over time
  • Works for homes and commercial buildings
  • Noise and heat concerns depend on the system and insulation

Roofing questions, answered

How do I know if my roof needs repair?

Look for missing or curling shingles, stains on ceilings, granules in gutters, or daylight in the attic. An inspection confirms what’s going on.

Do you inspect storm damage?

Yes. We document what we find with photos and notes you can share with your insurer.

What roofing materials do you install?

Asphalt shingles and metal roofing for homes, plus systems for commercial properties.

How does an estimate work?

We inspect first, then send a written scope and price so you can compare with confidence.
